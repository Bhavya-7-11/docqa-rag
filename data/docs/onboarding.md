# Acme Cloud Onboarding Guide

## First week
Your laptop is issued on day 1. Every new hire is assigned an onboarding buddy who answers day-to-day questions during the first month.

## 30-60-90 plan
Your manager will agree a 30-60-90 day plan with you in the first week. Progress is reviewed at the end of each period.

## Probation
The probation period is 3 months. A review meeting takes place in the final week of probation.

## Working hours
Core hours are 11:00 to 16:00 local time, when everyone is expected to be available. Outside core hours you may schedule your day flexibly.
