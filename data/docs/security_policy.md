# Acme Cloud Security Policy

## Accounts and passwords
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for every company account.

## Devices
All company laptops must use full-disk encryption. Lost or stolen devices must be reported immediately.

## Incident reporting
Any suspected security incident must be reported to security@acme.example within 1 hour of discovery.

## Access reviews
Access to production systems is reviewed quarterly by the security team. Unused access is removed.
