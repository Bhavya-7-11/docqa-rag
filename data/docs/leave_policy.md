# Acme Cloud Leave Policy

## Annual leave
Every full-time employee receives 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Up to 5 unused days can be carried over into the next year; anything above that expires on 31 March.

## Sick leave
Employees get 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Parental leave
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks.

## Requesting leave
Submit requests through the HR portal. Leave longer than 3 days must be requested at least 2 weeks in advance and approved by your manager.
